## Modular

`create-newt-app` is a new way to build applications based on modern TypeScript
principles. Each newt-app is structured for *Modular Monolith development*, where
the bulk of your business logic is framework-agnostic and organized across
multiple focused packages.

When your application is organized into multiple `packages`, you can expose
their features with `apps`. By default, we use Next.js, the leading React
framework, for the default app. However, the choice is yours. You can decide how
to compose and structure your system across `apps` and `packages`.

## Less is more

Unlike other project generators, we explicility do not offer many choices, nor do
we come *out-of-the-box* with lots of features.

- We do not install lots of dependencies.
- We do not support different databases.
- We do not prescribe lots of code.

We want users to understand their new project. To own their new project. `create-newt-app` ships with the bare minimum to get you started.
