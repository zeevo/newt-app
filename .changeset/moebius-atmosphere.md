Retheme the docs site with a Moebius-inspired palette and type system. Light mode is sun-bleached sand with deep umber ink; dark mode is night desert. Adds Instrument Serif as the display face, wires up the previously-undefined `--font-heading` token, and introduces `.grain`, `.vignette`, `.eyebrow`, and `.rule` utilities plus a pale-turquoise `--signal` accent. Corners are flattened from `0.625rem` to `0.25rem`.
